# Plume (fka Plume Design)

> Plume is a cloud-based smart-home and network-services platform for internet service providers (ISPs). Its offerings — Plume Home (residential WiFi, security, and parental controls), WorkPass (small business), Uprise (MDU), plus the Haystack and Sweepr operations/support tools — are delivered to hundreds of ISPs and millions of homes. Plume stewards OpenSync, an open-source, software-defined networking framework whose Northbound APIs let ISPs build their own cloud and applications. Public developer/API access is partner-gated (ISP and OpenSync certified-partner program); there is no open public OpenAPI specification.
